Ghloddish Temple is small misc entity in Evros that helps the small ghlodd minority on Ansur. Its questline would be primarily doing small, atmospheric chores that give you insight into the culture.

Carry the Dead
Head of the Temple informed you that one of older ghlodds in Evros died. They were one of the traditionalists and requested “the sacred travel” to the lands of their birth - At’har - to be buried in a cave cemetery under it.
Temple has contact with ship captain who quite frequently travels to Seiteh. The goal for you is to take the remains of the ghlodd and bring them safely to the ship and contact captain.
After that return to Temple Head to report back from captain.
