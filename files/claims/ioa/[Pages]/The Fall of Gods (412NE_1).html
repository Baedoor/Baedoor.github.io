I think one of the more fun easter eggs that could be added is The Fall of Gods magazine, 1st issue of 412 NE (look at "Oko Chaosu" in old Baedoor Encyclopaedia for explanation and details).

Reason?
Not only revealing a lot of interesting lore related to West Baedoor, but also being rare treat considering it's old piece of paper, so its appearance will be a bit unique.

Item has quest related to it, #48 being the only way to gather the piece. Applying it so early in game will make books/magazines appear early as well, kinda introducing players to the concept.
