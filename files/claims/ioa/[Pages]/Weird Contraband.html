Description:
On tutorial ship, there could be a barrel with hidden Lefs 8T. As much of a rare treat this will be, when you leave the ship in Ansur docks, there will be revision for some specific goods done. This will run only if you took Lefs 8T from the barrel, as this will point at you being one of contraband members.
Then, story will lead you directly to the governor, in which you will need to prove your innocence, and possibly, search for guilty people - this could go as far as to getting interview with each of ship members, but I'm not sure if it should get that far.

When we end the story, we will be freed, and if contraband is found due to your interviews, you will be given 50-300 pounds.
