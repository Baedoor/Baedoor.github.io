Description:
When on deck, sailor will tell you he's hungry. He will tell you that he is currently on guard, so can't really go downstairs, but he'd be super thankful for bringing him sweet rolls from kitches that he can smell from there.
Player will be able to convince cook to give roll away (should be very easy task) or steal. In case of failed steal though, it should fail quest as cook will not want to help you. There should be no penalty for failure here, just locking you out of trying again.

Reward up to claimant. The quest is port of BSaL quest to IoA. Also serves as tutorial quest.
