Activated charcoal, used in powder form with sugar, as a treat. Initially by pahtri, it spred to latoka, voitri, and later by saphtri migration to erds and kehts alike. Nowadays can be found in practically every corner of Baedoor Continent, to lesser or bigger extent depending on cultural trail from pahtris. Rather cheap. Might have variant that has colourful sprinkles, or one that is more packed, akin to medical tablets. Texture should remind the tablets anyway!
