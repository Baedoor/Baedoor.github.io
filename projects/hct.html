HermitCraft Tracker was made as an answer to how overwhelming it was for me to track all ~20 hermits doing their own HermitCraft episodes during Season 9. So, the app made was meant to allow me to write down every episode (sadly not releasing me from the sheer need of tracking videos down) and categorise by its status.

The app was made and improved not long after Season 10 started, but its functionality allows tracking and adding episodes from any season, from any hermit.
In the future when Librerian app is made and working with custom modules, there may be a converter made that will allow migrating HermitCraft Tracker entries to become part of Librerian system.
