Design documentation

Of Lands is turn-based strategy game inspired by classic 4X games and city builders.
The game would however put focus more on economics and survival, making player struggle more against environment, resource management and organisation than armies. Deemphasizing expansion is meant to offer different way to approach strategy, but also immerse you more into the world.
Domination and combat will remain, albeit are meant to be extremely challenging and not a major focus.

OL's development started at the end of September 2025, as at that moment the burnout from other projects made me rethink the old idea from different angle, eventually giving birth to what Of Lands is currently.
