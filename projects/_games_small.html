Ilmalaiva

Release date: 2023.07.07

Framework: Go

Simple arcade game built on premise of keeping airship afloat by constantly delivering fuel to its engine. Each few times you delivered the fuel you could earn some boost to help you with the task.
The game was made as entry for 2023's LCC "In the Sky" gamejam using Go's Ebiten framework.
It's also first of my finished games that support basic modding features, such as being able to create your own levels.

Near Risk of Death

Release date: 2023.06.06

Framework: Nim

Post-apocalyptic roleplaying game utilising Nim's Nigui framework, created together with skeletontonguedworld who made art for all locations.
Game was heavily based upon fight, as a player you could enhance your weapons and armour by whatever you found and sold from killed monsters.

Between Shadows and Light

Release date: 2018.07.19

Languages:

Framework: Python

First somewhat bigger game, yet still written in terminal and using fairly primitive Python code.
It can be seen as first appearance of Baedoor universe in digital, albeit at the time it wasn't public.
BSaL was roleplaying game, allowing you to visit Ansur island. It offered fairly engaging tutorial on the ship, ending with fighting pirates and landing on the island where you could do few quests, move freely between locations, sleep, buy newspaper and trade other items, break into warehouses as part of the thieves guild quest, or even make bank account and deposit money.

Temple Settlers

Release date: 2018.03.20

Languages:

Framework: Python

Town-building terminal game written in Python. Starting from mere resource gathering, you could expand the town to gather more resources or enable new features. Eventually, all of that was meant to prepare town for next waves of attacks - once the final attack is defeated, the game is won, allowing you to turn it into sandbox mode.

WarCards

Release date: 2018.03.12

Languages:

Framework: Python

First game project I made, being simple terminal cardgame written in Python. You drafted one of three warrior cards and could enhance your deck or play it. Depending on your decisions, you could survive the opponent's attack or not, in latter case your health was depleted.
